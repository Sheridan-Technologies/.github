# Sheridan Technologies

**Integrated product development for complex hardware, embedded systems, software, and AI-enabled products.**

Sheridan Technologies is an engineering and product development partner for teams building or rescuing complex products. We help clients design, integrate, validate, and ship hardware, embedded firmware, software, and connected systems with manufacturability, reliability, and real-world delivery in mind.

We are especially useful when the work is multidisciplinary, high-stakes, behind schedule, technically blocked, or difficult to coordinate across vendors and specialists.

## What we do

- Product development from concept to prototype to production
- Embedded firmware and real-time systems
- Electronics engineering, PCB design, schematics, bring-up, and debugging
- Software development, tooling, dashboards, interfaces, and integrations
- AI integration, automation, and computer vision where it creates practical value
- Systems integration across hardware, firmware, software, suppliers, and manufacturing
- Project rescue, stabilization, and technical risk reduction

## What makes Sheridan different

- **One accountable technical lead.** We coordinate the moving pieces so clients are not stuck managing handoffs between disconnected vendors.
- **Senior expert network.** We bring in the right specialists for the phase and problem, without the overhead of a large consultancy.
- **Built for hard problems.** We work across hardware, firmware, software, test, integration, and production readiness.
- **Risk-aware execution.** We surface tradeoffs early, make decisions with evidence, and design for reliability, testability, and manufacturability.
- **Trusted-advisor mindset.** We lead with honesty. If we are not the right fit, we will say so.
